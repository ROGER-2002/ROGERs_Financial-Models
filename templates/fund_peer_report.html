Why some values are N/A

Forward Ratio needs an agreed definition and forward estimates. Benchmark metrics need a selected benchmark and overlapping observations. Period metrics and ranks can be unavailable where a fund lacks sufficient NAV history for the selected window. The app shows these fields as N/A rather than estimating them.
