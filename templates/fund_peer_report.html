Metric availability and interpretation

Expense ratio is not in the supplied scheme-level data. Portfolio turnover requires transaction flows and average net assets; holdings snapshots alone do not provide the official rate. Forward Ratio needs an agreed definition and forward estimates. Benchmark metrics need a selected benchmark and overlapping observations. Period metrics and ranks can be unavailable where a fund lacks sufficient NAV history for the selected window. The app shows these fields as N/A rather than estimating them.

Returns and risk measures in this report use monthly observations. Check the methodology notes in the project documentation for calculation conventions, coverage rules, and limitations.
